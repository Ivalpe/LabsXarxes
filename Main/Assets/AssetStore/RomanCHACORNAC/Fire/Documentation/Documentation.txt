--------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------
A stylized, fully opaque campfire built for mobile and VR.

- No transparency: no overdraw, no sorting issues, correct
  depth with every object around it.
- One tiny unlit shader, no texture sampling.
- GPU instanced particles: the whole fire costs a few draw
  calls.
- No setup: drop a prefab in your scene and you are done.
- All colors live in the particle systems, so you can restyle
  the fire without touching any code or shader.

--------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------
Unity        : Unity 6 (6000.0) or newer
Pipelines    : URP: Yes   Built-in: Yes   HDRP: No
Platforms    : PC, Mobile (Android / iOS), VR (single-pass instanced)
Shader       : RomanCHACORNAC/VFX/Uber

The demo scene is made for URP (Linear color space, Bloom in
a Volume). In Built-in, drop the prefabs in your own scene.

Built-in only: P_FireLight shows a "Missing Script" component
(URP light settings, added by Unity). It has no effect on the
light: select P_FireLight and remove the empty component, or
simply ignore it.

--------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK START
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the demo scene: RomanCHACORNAC/Fire/Demo/Fire_Demo.unity
2. Drag one of the prefabs into your scene:
   - P_Campfire    : fire, light, fireflies and logs
   - VFX_Fire      : the fire alone
   - P_FireLight   : the flickering fire light alone
   - VFX_Fireflies : the floating fireflies alone
   All prefabs are in RomanCHACORNAC/Fire/Prefabs.
3. Press Play. No setup needed.

Tip: the fire looks best with Bloom enabled in your Volume.
The material Base Color is HDR: raise it to make the fire glow.

--------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------
VFX_Fire is made of 3 particle systems:

  Base       The bright core at the bottom of the fire.
  Flames     The body of the flames, rising and shrinking.
  Sparks     The sparks and embers flying out.

P_Campfire adds:

  P_FireLight    A warm point light with a soft flicker.
  VFX_Fireflies  Small glowing dots floating around.
  Log_1 to 3     Two modeled log meshes.

Every particle is opaque: small meshes for Base and Flames,
opaque quads for Sparks and Fireflies. Instead of fading
out, particles shrink to zero (Size over Lifetime): this is
what keeps the effect fully opaque and cheap.

--------------------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Color       Start Color and Color over Lifetime, on each
            particle system. The shader multiplies them by the
            material Base Color.
Glow        Material Base Color (HDR). Higher values = more
            Bloom.
Size        Scale the prefab: the particles follow (Scaling
            Mode: Hierarchy). For finer control, Start Size
            and Size over Lifetime.
Height      Start Lifetime and Start Speed of Flames.
Density     Emission > Rate over Time. Keep Max Particles low on
            mobile.
Wind        Velocity over Lifetime or Force over Lifetime.
Light       P_FireLight: color, intensity, range and shadows on
            the Light. Fire Light Flicker (same object) varies
            them around these values: flicker amount, range
            amount and speed. Remove it for a steady light.
            Disable the shadows, or remove the light, on
            low-end devices.
Shapes      Renderer > Mesh on Base and Flames. Any mesh works;
            the included one is in RomanCHACORNAC/Fire/Meshes.

Good to know:
- Keep "Enable GPU Instancing" checked on the renderers.
- Billboard and Stretched Billboard modes also work with the
  shader.

--------------------------------------------------------------
PERFORMANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------
- VFX_Fire: 89 particles at most, 3 particle systems.
- P_Campfire: 125 particles at most (fireflies included),
  4 particle systems, GPU instanced: about 4 draw calls for
  the effect, plus the logs.
- Opaque and depth-tested: no overdraw, no transparency sort.
- Unlit shader, no texture sampling.
- 1 point light with soft shadows: the most expensive part.
  Turn its shadows off on mobile.

--------------------------------------------------------------
UPGRADING FROM FULL OPAQUE FIRE (previous version)
--------------------------------------------------------------
This update is a full rewrite. To upgrade:
1. Back up your project.
2. Delete the old Assets/FullOpaqueFire folder.
3. Import this package.

The fires already placed in your scenes are then replaced by
the new VFX_Fire automatically. Their old overrides (colors,
VFX_FireController settings) no longer apply: check them and
adjust the new particle systems if needed.

Breaking changes:
- New folder: Assets/RomanCHACORNAC/Fire.
- The VFX_FireController script was removed. Color, intensity
  and wind are now set directly on the particle systems.
- New shader RomanCHACORNAC/VFX/Uber replaces S_Particle.
- Unity 6 is now required.
